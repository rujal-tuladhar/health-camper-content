Health Camper — Support

Welcome to the campsite. Answers to the common questions are below; for anything else, email rujal.tuladhar@gmail.com and you'll get a reply from a real person.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no paywall, no ads, no account.

Do the sounds need internet?

No. Every sound — campfire, rain, wind, river, crickets, sleep noise — is generated live on your iPhone. Airplane mode works fine.

Sounds keep playing when I lock the phone?

Yes, that's intentional — soundscapes are made for sleeping. Stop them from the app or the Lock Screen controls.

How do I sync with Apple Health?

Settings inside the app → enable Health sync, then allow Mindful Minutes when iOS asks. The app only writes your quiet minutes; it never reads your health data.

How do I delete my data?

Everything lives on your phone, so deleting the app deletes all of it.

Something's broken

Email a screenshot and your iPhone model to rujal.tuladhar@gmail.com — bugs get fixed fast.
