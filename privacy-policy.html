Health Camper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

The short version: Health Camper collects no data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

Your sessions, moods, journal entries, goals, streaks and settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple Health (optional)

If you turn on Health sync and grant permission, the app writes your completed quiet minutes to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes, on your device. The app never reads your health data, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can revoke this anytime in the Health app.

Your photo library (optional)

If you pick a personal motivation video, the app copies it into its own private storage on your device. It is never uploaded.

The daily campfire feed

The app fetches a small public text file (daily lines and links) from GitHub's servers. This is an anonymous download — the request contains no account, no identifier, and nothing about you. GitHub's servers see the same basic connection information any website visit involves; we never see even that.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics or crash-tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No selling, sharing, or transferring of data — we have nothing to sell.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
